Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about our deployment terms and operational safeguards.

How quickly can USF deploy guards to our facility?

For standard deployments, we complete the preliminary site inspection and deploy vetted, uniformed guards within 24 to 48 hours. For emergency or short-term event needs, we can mobilize priority squads rapidly.

What happens if a guard falls ill or misses a shift?

We maintain an active reserve squad ready for immediate dispatch. If a guard is unable to stand duty, a briefed replacement officer is deployed without leaving your post unattended.

Can we combine security guards with housekeeping staff?

Yes. Many of our corporate and showroom clients take advantage of our integrated facility service, combining manned security guards with our uniformed housekeeping attendants under a single consolidated contract.

How do you ensure night-shift guards stay awake and alert?

Through randomized, surprise physical inspections conducted by mobile field officers, hourly logbook check-in requirements, and disciplined shift handovers.
